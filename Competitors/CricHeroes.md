# What Exactly Is CricHeroes?

CricHeroes is essentially the **operating system for grassroots cricket**.

Think of it as:

|Industry|Equivalent|
|---|---|
|Cricket|CricHeroes|
|Football|Transfermarkt + Local League Management|
|Running|Strava|
|Chess|Chess.com|
|Coding|GitHub|

Its primary job is:

**Capture cricket data.**

Not discover talent.

Not run scouting.

Not verify players.

Just collect and manage cricket activity.

---

# What CricHeroes Actually Does

When a local cricket tournament happens:

- Someone creates a tournament
    
- Teams register
    
- Matches are scheduled
    
- Scorers enter ball-by-ball data
    
- Scorecards are generated
    
- Statistics are tracked
    

CricHeroes handles all of this.

---

### 1. Digital Scoring

Instead of paper scorebooks:

- Every run recorded

People can watch score updates live.

### 5. Video Streaming

### 7. Academy Listings

# Why CricHeroes Became Huge

The reason is simple:

Before CricHeroes, grassroots cricket data barely existed.

Most tournaments used:

- Paper scorebooks
    
- WhatsApp groups
    
- Excel sheets
    

CricHeroes digitized all of it.

The result:

- Millions of matches
    
- Millions of players
    
- Massive cricket database
    

As your document notes, CricHeroes has become one of the largest grassroots cricket databases globally.

# My Conclusion

CricHeroes is **not your direct competitor today**.

A better description is:

> CricHeroes is the data infrastructure layer.
> 
> SportX wants to become the talent intelligence and discovery layer built on top of that infrastructure.

However, CricHeroes is also the company most capable of becoming your direct competitor because they already own:

- The users
    
- The data
    
- The distribution
    
- The cricket ecosystem relationships
    

The safest strategic assumption is:

> Treat CricHeroes as a partner in the short term, but design SportX so that it can survive even if CricHeroes refuses API access or launches a competing scouting product in the future.

That assumption will force you to build a much stronger business.
