AthlasX is a two-sided talent discovery platform for Indian cricket. On one side, serious district-level cricketers build verified, data-rich profiles — linked to real scorecards, coach confirmations, and [[CricHeroes]] [[CricHeroes^]]match history. On the other side, state selectors, academy scouts, and pathway coaches get a searchable dashboard to find exactly the kind of player they need, filtered by role, age, district, and performance benchmarks.

The core problem AthlasX solves is visibility. A player averaging 55 runs per innings in Bihar or Assam is statistically elite — but completely invisible to anyone outside his local ecosystem. No selector is watching. No database surfaces him. His only path to a trial is knowing the right coach who knows the right person. AthlasX replaces that broken network with a merit-indexed system where performance speaks louder than connections.

The business model is B2B: state associations, academies, and franchise analysts pay for scout-side access. Players join free. The platform's moat is its verification layer — every profile carries an independently verified trust score, making it the only place where a scout can search with confidence that what they're seeing is real.
