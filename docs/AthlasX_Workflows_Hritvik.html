Overview & My Take on the Pivot

Mrigank's document is strong — the market research is solid and the workflow structure is the right framing. The buyer is the association. The wedge is the pre-camp dossier. The compounding loop is the business. I agree with all three.

Where I'd push back or add:

  • CricHeroes check must happen before everything. If the target association is already on CricHeroes (KCA is), the W1 ingest sprint is weeks not months. This single question changes the build plan more than anything else.
  • Phone number is the primary resolution key in W2 — not fuzzy name matching. UPCA mandates one phone per registration. Use it.
  • Print-first on dossiers. Trial grounds don't have reliable connectivity. Grading and dossier delivery must work offline. Design that first.
  • Phase 0 before Phase 1. Get one real tournament's raw data ingested end-to-end before building anything in W3/W4. Until that's done, every cost assumption is untested.
  • Revenue must be decided deliberately. Working assumption (pilot free → revenue from franchise scout access to district tier) needs to be an explicit decision, not a drift.
🔴 No association conversation is yet on record. All institutional assumptions are untested against a real buyer. This is the most important item in the validation backlog — everything else is premature without it.

My Take on W1

CricHeroes check is step zero. Kanpur Cricket Association already has a CricHeroes team profile with match history. Before any format audit, determine whether the target association is using CricHeroes or another scoring platform. If yes: the ingest pipeline collapses to an API sync. Cost drops 80%. Data quality is higher.

MOU needs a legal template before first outreach. One legal review of a template, then use it every time. Don't wing the data-sharing terms in the room.

The P1 decision (data format) must happen in meeting 1. This single decision sets the engineering timeline and the unit economics of the business. Don't commit to a delivery date before you know the answer.

association_verified status is the trust primitive. Everything the dossier and the AthlasX Score trade on comes from this flag. One bad season approval destroys trust with the selection panel.

🔴 Format audit is the single biggest cost driver. OCR + human review of paper scorebooks is months of work per season. If the association has paper records, budget explicitly for it before signing.
🔴 Confirm who controls export rights if the association is on a third-party platform. It may be the platform, not the association.

My Take on W2

Phone number is the primary key. UPCA's registration system mandates one phone number per registration. If that phone is in the ingest data, resolution is a lookup, not a fuzzy-matching problem. Ask for phone in the ingest data spec upfront — it simplifies the entire resolution engine.

Exception queue → association staff, not AthlasX ops. They know whether "Rahul Sharma, Lucknow U19" is the same person across two different tournament files. Routing ambiguous matches to us doesn't scale and we're not the authority. They are.

Shadow profiles are NOT publicly discoverable. Association-only view until the player claims and consents. This is both a DPDP Act requirement and a trust requirement with the association.

Guardian consent is a hard gate for under-18 — not a soft checkbox. A large share of this population is U16 and U14. Do not ship the claim flow without the guardian consent path fully implemented and tested.

Consent withdrawal must be immediate. One button. Not a support ticket. Not "we'll process your request in 30 days." Immediate delist and processing stop. Build this before launch.

🔴 Establish the guardian-consent and data-retention position with a lawyer before ingesting a single record at U14/U16 level. Not after.

My Take on W3

Print-first design. The pre-camp pack must have a printable PDF as the primary deliverable. Trial grounds (district level, zone level) do not have reliable connectivity. A panel member should be able to walk in with a printed pack and use it independently of any device or app. Design the paper version first, then the digital version.

Grading must work offline. Non-negotiable. If the grading app needs connectivity, it will not be used at real district trial grounds. Build with IndexedDB or local storage. Sync when online.

Deliver the pack the night before, not the morning of. The panel needs time to pre-read and flag players they want to watch closely. A pack delivered at 8am on camp day is functionally useless.

The thin dossier is a feature, not a gap. "First season on record" is the hook that makes the player claim their profile and add footage. Frame it that way in the UI — not as an empty page but as the start of a permanent record.

Footage upload: remove every barrier. 30–60 seconds, phone camera quality, no minimum resolution. A poorly lit clip of a real talent is more useful than no clip at all. Don't set requirements that exclude players with basic phones.

🔴 We have never co-designed a dossier with an actual sitting selector. The format needs at least one real selector's input before we build. Don't build this in a vacuum.

My Take on W4

Blind grading before convergence is non-negotiable. It prevents the loudest voice from anchoring the room. It surfaces genuine disagreement instead of manufactured consensus. A clean committee will actively want this feature. A captured committee will resist it — that resistance is a signal, not a problem to solve by removing the feature.

Show grade distribution, not just averages. A player graded 9/9/2 by three selectors is a different situation from a player graded 5/5/5. The disagreement is information. The convergence view must show both.

G8 (omission rationale) is off by default. This is the highest-value audit feature and the highest-risk sales objection. Recording why someone wasn't selected is politically sensitive in any association with selection irregularities — and many have them. The association must control this toggle themselves. We never ship it on.

The score is advisory, not decisive. Make this explicit in the UI. "Ranked by AthlasX Score as a starting point" — selectors should never feel the platform is making the decision for them.

The immutable record is the compounding mechanism. Each selection record makes next cycle's dossiers more accurate. After three cycles, the platform holds longitudinal selection history no external scout or database has.

🔴 Test the reaction to the audit feature (G8) before building it. Frame it as "something clean committees use to document their work." Never as accountability or external oversight.

My Take on W5

"Scorer already does this" is an assumption, not a verified fact. Validate per association whether their scorer uses CricHeroes or another structured platform before making any promise about auto-ingest. If not, manual entry is the fallback — and we need to own that UX properly so it actually gets used.

Coach input is optional enrichment, not a dependency. The weekly player card updates from match data alone. If the coach doesn't log in for a week, the cycle still completes. Don't block the pipeline on coach actions.

Notify the selector too, not just the coach. When an attention flag triggers, the selection panel needs to know — not just the coach. The selector is making next-cycle decisions and needs the trend data before that cycle opens.

4-week rolling average is the right threshold. Catches genuine performance slumps while ignoring normal week-to-week variation. Don't make the window shorter or it becomes noise.

My Take on W6

Default visibility is association-only — not opt-out. Players and parents of U16 players did not sign up to have franchise scouts browsing their child's profile. Default = association-only is both the right privacy position and the defensible one.

The first view must be valuable. Every match on file, with stats. If the first screen a player sees after claiming their profile is empty or generic, the claim rate will be near zero. The hook is: "here is your complete tournament history in one place — things you couldn't see anywhere else."

Franchise scout exposure (V3) is V2, not Phase 1. The association relationship must be established and trusted before we add a commercial layer for outside buyers accessing their district players.

Trial notification + registration deep link is the engagement loop. "UPCA U19 trials for Lucknow district open tomorrow. Registration fee ₹400. Direct link." Specific, timely, actionable. This is why players keep the app active.

Self-reported fitness = zero in score. Make this visible and explained in the UI. If players don't understand why logging their gym sessions doesn't move their score, they'll think the platform is broken.

Deliberately thin in Phase 1. The coach is a contributor to the record, not the primary customer. Don't over-invest here — this tier is where QloSport is strongest and our differentiation is weakest.

My Take on W7

Coach advises — does not decide. Permissions: read access to their squad's performance data, write access to their squad's session notes, advisory-only input into W4. No more than this in Phase 1.

Phase 1 scope is intentionally minimal. Attendance, session notes, post-match review. Everything else is V2. QloSport already does coach features well — we compete on the association relationship and the selection workflow, not on whether our attendance log has more fields than theirs.

Session note format must be constrained. 200 characters max. If the field is unlimited, coaches write nothing or write novels. Neither is useful in a dossier. One sentence per player, per week.

🔴 QloSport's coach product is fully built and in production. Don't compete on coach feature parity in Phase 1 — the differentiation is at the association / selection layer, not the coaching layer.

My Take on W8

Build the data pipeline from Day 1, even though the academy product is Phase 3+. The academy name field is in every player row we ingest. If we don't extract and track it from the first ingest, we lose the longitudinal data that makes the ranked academy view credible. Don't skip W8 in the ingest spec.

Academy name reconciliation will be the most labour-intensive part. Names will come in as: "SCA", "Sharma Cricket Academy", "Sharma CA Lucknow", "Sharma Cricket Academy (Lucknow Branch)". Budget explicitly for a reconciliation pass after every new association's first ingest.

Calibrate the 0.85 threshold on real data. Prefer false negatives (unmatched stub) over false positives (merging two different academies). A merged duplicate is harder to unwind than an unmatched entry.

"Which academies actually produce district-level players" is a genuinely novel asset. No one currently holds this, evidenced rather than claimed. After 2 seasons of ingest, the pitch to an academy is: "28 of your past players appeared in district trials last season. We can show you who they are, how they performed, and where they went." That's a reason to sign up — we don't need to sell them.

🟢 Inbound academy signup (Y10) means we don't have to run a thousands-wide outbound sales motion to academies. The data accumulates silently, then they come to us. This is the right acquisition model for the academy tier.

My Take on W9

Phase 0 before anything. Get one real tournament's raw data from one district association, ingested end-to-end, with a known engineering cost. Until that's done, every cost assumption in this business is untested and every timeline commitment is a guess.

Revenue must be decided deliberately. Working assumption: pilot is free for data. Revenue comes from franchise scout and state-level access to the district player tier. If we don't decide this explicitly, it will decide itself badly — probably we give away the product and never establish a revenue motion.

Academy tier is inbound, not outbound. Don't run an outbound sales motion to academies in Phase 1. Let the data accumulate. The ranked academy view brings them to us. This is a better acquisition model than thousands of cold outreach calls to cricket academies.
